Best Picture

 

An answer print is the first finished copy of a film, picture and sound together, made for the filmmakers to approve before it goes out to theaters.

Winners and nominations come from Wikidata, a free public database. Ratings come from OMDb. Film images come from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. A brand-new winner can take a few days to show up.
