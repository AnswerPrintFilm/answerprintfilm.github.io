Every Oscar winner since the very first ceremony, in one clean place. Pick an award on the reel to see its full history, or search any film for its ratings, its story, and the Oscars it won.

Best Picture

 

An answer print is the first finished copy of a film, picture and sound together, made for the filmmakers to approve before it goes out to theaters.

Winners and nominations come from Wikidata, a free public database. Ratings and posters come from OMDb. A brand-new winner can take a few days to show up.
