    About Answer Print

    A one-stop shop for film history, optics, and specs.

    Answer Print brings together the details film lovers usually have to hunt for across several sites. Search any film to see its ratings, its story, every Academy Award it won or was nominated for, and how it was shot. Or browse every Academy Award winner, back to the first ceremony in 1929.

    Why “Answer Print”

    In a film lab, the answer print is the first finished copy of a film, with picture and sound together, made so the filmmakers can approve the lab's work before copies go out to theaters. The name is a nod to that moment, when everything about a film finally comes together in one place.

    What you'll find

    • The five most popular films right now, in theaters and on streaming
    • Every Academy Award winner in 24 categories, back to the first ceremony
    • Ratings from IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, and Metacritic
    • Each film's story, director, writers, and cast
    • Every Academy Award a film won or was nominated for
    • How a film was shot, when it's on record: the film format, camera, and aspect ratio

    Where the information comes from

    Awards and format details come from Wikidata, a free, community-maintained database. Ratings, plots, and credits come from OMDb, and film images and the popular lists come from TMDB. Because these sources are kept up by people, a detail is occasionally missing or mistaken, and a brand-new winner can take a few days to appear.
